## What works

PostgreSQL writes account or order state and its notification event in the same transaction. A restart-safe worker leases pending outbox rows, creates an idempotent in-app item, records delivery attempts, and safely replays expired leases. Unknown or privacy-invalid events are dead-lettered instead of displayed.

The inbox supports unread and read state, mark-all-read, category filters, persistence across restarts, and owner-scoped access. Empty, loading, sign-in-required, and error states are visible in the product.

## Honest completion language

An order status alone does not produce a success message. The “verified final receipt” notification is emitted only when a genuine receipt row is committed. Detection, confirmation, payout submission, refund review, failure, unresolved, expiry, and cancellation each retain distinct wording.

## Redaction boundary

Inbox payloads reject wallet addresses, recipients, signatures, transaction hashes, transaction IDs, and email addresses. The item may link the owner back to private activity, where normal authorization runs again.

<DocNotice title="External alerts remain disabled" tone="warning">
  Email, push, SMS, webhooks, and public notification feeds are not implemented. No private transaction data is sent to an external alert provider.
</DocNotice>
