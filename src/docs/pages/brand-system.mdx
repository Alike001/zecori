## Name and tagline

Zecori is the provisional customer-facing product name. The product promise is “Private origin. Verified arrival.” Repository directory, package identifiers, environment-variable prefixes, and database names remain unchanged for compatibility and provenance.

## Mark meaning

A concealed circular origin meets a vertical privacy boundary, then becomes a split route ending in two public evidence points. The mark avoids generic Z, shield, and chain-link motifs and makes no claim of trustlessness.

## Palette and motion

Zcash-inspired gold and evidence teal carry route state against dark ink and warm ivory surfaces. Route-path motion communicates direction without simulating settlement and is removed when reduced motion is requested.

## Naming gate

Provisional adoption is not trademark or domain clearance. Before external launch, search exact and phonetic variants in relevant registries, companies, app stores, social handles, and domains, then document jurisdiction and confusion risk.
