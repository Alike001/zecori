## Verified now

Guest exploration, passkey accounts, deterministic route answers, owner-scoped lifecycle state, and redacted receipts have automated evidence.

## Experimental

The fixed test route, destination proof, exact-match handles, encrypted private contacts, non-paying handle request intents, durable in-app inbox, Groq chat adapter, and speech gateway exist in code. A separate default-off test engine covers future variable atomic amounts, approved test rates, fees, bounds, capacity, expiry, exact binding, and rounding; it is not wired into the product. Provider-backed AI, microphone/TTS, French/Arabic security copy, and funded chain behavior retain explicit live or human acceptance work. Zecori is adopted provisionally as the customer-facing name; legal clearance remains open.

## Disabled

Privy, payment by handle, variable amounts, external alerts, live quotes, automatic settlement/refunds, and mainnet stay off. Privy remains blocked by unresolved dependency advisories; no unsupported Zod override is permitted.

## Future route expansion

The product direction is direct, liquidity-backed payouts to additional destination networks and tokens. No new chain or token is shown as supported until its registry, exact terms, recipient proof, verifier, liquidity, signing separation, recovery, and receipt gates are implemented and tested.
