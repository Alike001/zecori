## What stays private

The Zcash source uses the Ironwood shielded pool. Private account history and destination data are owner-scoped, and stored settlement evidence is encrypted at rest. Public receipts redact source correlation. Optional contact labels are encrypted and owner-scoped; handles expose no address, order history, or contact graph.

## Handle and inbox privacy

Handle lookup is exact-match, opt-in, rate-limited, and deliberately returns the same unavailable result for reserved and claimed names. Inbox event payloads reject address, recipient, signature, transaction-hash, transaction-ID, and email fields. External notification delivery is disabled.

## What becomes public

The Base Sepolia destination transaction is transparent: recipient, token amount, and transaction hash are public on the destination chain. Identity, wallet, and RPC providers may also observe metadata.

## Trusted LP custody

The experimental trusted liquidity provider and authorized verification services see evidence required for settlement. The LP temporarily controls received test funds. Payouts require separate approval and verification; refunds are manual and not guaranteed.
