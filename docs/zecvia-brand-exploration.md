# Zecvia provisional brand exploration

Status: `EXPERIMENTAL · NOT CLEARED`. Zecathon remains the active product, repository, account, and domain name.

## Strategic idea

Zecvia combines a Zcash cue with “via”: a guarded route through privacy and evidence boundaries. The visual system must communicate calm coordination and verifiable outcomes without suggesting an automatic or trustless bridge.

The three original directions are:

1. **Veiled Route** — a Z-shaped path crosses a protected circle. Gold is the shielded source; cyan is destination evidence.
2. **Quiet Relay** — two offset nodes and a restrained handoff line emphasize coordination across distinct systems.
3. **Evidence Window** — paired frames contrast an obscured source view with a verified output view. This is the strongest candidate for a UI system.

Implementation-ready vectors live in `public/brand-exploration/`. The generated identity board is `zecvia-concept-board.png`. These assets are exploratory and are not loaded into the current Zecathon header, favicon, metadata, or product name.

## Preliminary naming screen

An exact-name web search on 2026-10-26 found no obvious active product named “Zecvia.” It did find **Zeqvia**, an active AI automation business at [zeqvia.com](https://zeqvia.com/). The spelling and pronunciation are close enough to require a professional confusion analysis. Search-engine absence is not evidence of trademark availability.

The USPTO provides an official [Trademark Search system](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/search) and recommends searching similar marks and likelihood of confusion. Before any selection or public use:

- define launch jurisdictions and goods/services;
- search exact, phonetic, prefix, suffix, and design variants in USPTO, WIPO Global Brand Database, EUIPO, UKIPO, and relevant local registries;
- review at least Nice classes 9, 36, and 42 with counsel;
- search corporate registries, app stores, package registries, social handles, crypto projects, and press;
- obtain current registrar/registry results for target domains immediately before purchase;
- document legal advice, conflicts, coexistence risk, and the final selection decision.

No domain was purchased, no account was renamed, and no trademark conclusion is claimed.
